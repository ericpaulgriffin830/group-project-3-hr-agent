======================================================================
LEAVE-1 Types of Leave
======================================================================
Northbridge provides several categories of leave beyond standard PTO
(see PTO-HOLIDAYS): family and medical leave, parental leave, non-FMLA
medical leave, bereavement leave, and jury duty / civic leave. Each
type has its own eligibility rules and documentation requirements,
described in the sections below. Leave under this policy runs
concurrently with any leave required under applicable state or federal
law, including the federal Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) and any
state family leave program, rather than in addition to it. Where a
state law provides a broader right than described in this policy (for
example, a longer job-protection period, or eligibility for a smaller
employer than the federal FMLA threshold), Northbridge follows the
broader state requirement for employees working in that state.

Leave under this policy is distinct from, and should not be confused
with, the temporary remote work location changes described in the
Remote Work Policy; an employee who needs extended time away from work
entirely should use this policy, while an employee who wants to
continue working but from a different location should use the Remote
Work Policy instead.

======================================================================
LEAVE-2 Family and Medical Leave (FMLA-aligned)
======================================================================
Employees who have worked at Northbridge for at least 12 months and
at least 1,250 hours in the preceding 12 months are eligible for up to
12 weeks of job-protected leave per year for their own serious health
condition, to care for an immediate family member with a serious
health condition, or for the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a
child. This leave is unpaid unless covered by PTO or short-term
disability benefits (see BEN-6), which employees may use concurrently
with FMLA leave to receive pay during the leave period. Group health
benefits continue during FMLA leave on the same terms as active
employment, meaning the employee continues to pay their normal share
of premiums and the Company continues its normal contribution, exactly
as if the employee were actively working.

The 12-week entitlement is measured on a rolling 12-month basis looked
back from the start of any FMLA leave, consistent with federal
regulations; an employee who has already used 5 weeks of FMLA leave in
the past 12 months has 7 weeks remaining, not a fresh 12-week
entitlement, until enough time has passed for the earlier leave to
roll off the 12-month lookback window.

======================================================================
LEAVE-3 Parental Leave
======================================================================
In addition to any FMLA leave under LEAVE-2, Northbridge provides 6
weeks of fully paid parental leave to all employees (regardless of the
12-month FMLA eligibility threshold) following the birth, adoption, or
foster placement of a child. Paid parental leave runs concurrently
with FMLA leave for eligible employees and is available on a
standalone basis for employees not yet FMLA-eligible. Parental leave
must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event and may be
taken continuously or, with manager and HR agreement, in up to two
blocks of at least 2 weeks each. Both parents employed by Northbridge
following the same qualifying event (for example, two Northbridge
employees who are partners welcoming a child together) are each
independently eligible for the full 6 weeks of paid parental leave.

======================================================================
LEAVE-4 Medical Leave (Non-FMLA)
======================================================================
Employees not yet eligible for FMLA leave under LEAVE-2, or who need
leave beyond the 12-week FMLA entitlement, may request additional
unpaid medical leave of up to 8 additional weeks, subject to People
Operations approval and medical documentation. This leave is not
automatically job-protected in the way FMLA leave is, though
Northbridge makes reasonable efforts to hold the employee's position
or offer a comparable role upon return, consistent with applicable
disability accommodation law. Requests for medical leave beyond this
additional 8-week period are evaluated individually by People
Operations and Legal, generally in connection with an ongoing
interactive accommodation process under applicable disability law
rather than as a standard leave extension.

======================================================================
LEAVE-5 Bereavement Leave
======================================================================
Employees receive up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave following the
death of an immediate family member (spouse, domestic partner, child,
parent, or sibling), and up to 2 paid days for the death of an
extended family member (grandparent, grandchild, aunt, uncle, or
in-law). Additional unpaid time or use of PTO may be arranged with
manager approval for travel or extended circumstances, particularly
where the employee needs to travel internationally or handle estate
matters that extend beyond the standard bereavement leave period.

======================================================================
LEAVE-6 Jury Duty and Civic Leave
======================================================================
Northbridge provides paid leave for jury duty for the full duration of
service, and unpaid leave (or PTO, at the employee's election) for
voting time and other civic obligations to the extent required by
applicable state law. Employees should provide their jury summons to
their manager and People Operations as soon as it is received, both so
that People Operations can confirm the applicable paid-leave treatment
and so that the manager can plan for the employee's absence during the
jury service period, which can vary significantly in length depending
on the case.

======================================================================
LEAVE-7 Requesting Leave and Documentation
======================================================================
Leave requests are submitted through an HR ticket in the "Leave
Request" category (see HR-OPS-2), with a target initial response time
of 2 business days. Medical certification is required for FMLA leave
(LEAVE-2) and non-FMLA medical leave (LEAVE-4) using the standard
medical certification form provided by People Operations. Parental
leave (LEAVE-3) and bereavement leave (LEAVE-5) do not require medical
certification but do require basic supporting documentation (such as a
birth certificate, adoption record, or obituary) within 30 days of the
leave starting, submitted confidentially to People Operations per
HR-OPS-4.

Employees may use available PTO to cover some or all of the unpaid
portion of an approved leave (for example, the portion of FMLA leave
not otherwise covered by paid parental leave or short-term disability),
as referenced in PTO-8. Employees who wish to do this should indicate
their preference when submitting the leave request, since PTO usage
during leave is not automatic and requires the employee's election.

======================================================================
LEAVE-8 Job Protection and Return to Work
======================================================================
Employees on FMLA-eligible leave (LEAVE-2) or paid parental leave
(LEAVE-3) are restored to the same or an equivalent position upon
return, consistent with federal and state law. Employees returning
from a medical leave involving a serious health condition may be asked
to provide a fitness-for-duty certification before resuming work,
particularly where the role involves safety-sensitive duties or
client-facing responsibilities. Questions about return-to-work
accommodations should be directed to People Operations through an HR
ticket well before the planned return date, so that any necessary
accommodation (such as a modified schedule for a gradual return) can be
arranged in advance rather than on the employee's first day back.

======================================================================
LEAVE-9 Interaction with Other Policies
======================================================================
Leave under this policy interacts with several other documents in this
corpus: PTO-8 describes how PTO can be used to cover unpaid leave
portions; BEN-3 and BEN-6 describe how benefits and short-term
disability continue during leave; and HR-OPS-4 describes the
confidentiality protections that apply to leave-related medical
documentation, which is held to a stricter standard than most other HR
records and is not shared with an employee's manager beyond the fact
that leave has been approved and its expected duration. An employee
whose leave need arises from a workplace safety incident should also
review the incident reporting expectations referenced in the
Information Security Policy (SEC-6) and the Workplace Conduct Policy
(CONDUCT-7) where relevant, since those sections address the reporting
process for the underlying incident separately from the leave itself.
