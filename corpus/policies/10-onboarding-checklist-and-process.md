# Onboarding Checklist and Process

## ONB-1 Pre-Start Preparation

People Operations initiates the onboarding workflow as soon as an offer is signed. This includes background check initiation, I-9 and tax withholding form assignment through the HR portal, and equipment provisioning request to IT (see EQ-1) so that a laptop ships to the new hire's registered work location at least 3 business days before their start date. Managers are notified of their new hire's start date and are expected to complete ONB-5 before day one. People Operations also confirms at this stage that the new hire's registered work location is a Registered State under TAX-2, flagging any exception to Finance before the start date if it is not.

## ONB-2 Day One Checklist

On the first day, new hires should: complete remaining I-9 verification (in person at a Company office or through a remote notary service for fully remote hires), gain access to core systems (email, HR portal, VPN, and department-specific tools), complete the Information Security acknowledgment referencing INFOSEC, and meet with their manager for a role overview. IT support is available same-day for any equipment or access issues, and new hires are encouraged to report any access problem immediately rather than working around it, both so it can be fixed quickly and because working around an access restriction can itself raise a SEC-2 acceptable-use question.

## ONB-3 First Week Checklist

During the first week, new hires should complete benefits enrollment information review (formal elections are not due until the deadline in BEN-3), review the Workplace Conduct policy (CONDUCT) and acknowledge receipt, review the PTO and Holidays policy (PTO-HOLIDAYS) and confirm their work arrangement classification (onsite, hybrid, or remote) with their manager, which determines home office stipend eligibility under EXP-2. New hires in a hybrid or remote arrangement should also review the Remote Work Policy (REMOTE-WORK) during this week, so they understand the approval process for any future location changes before a need for one arises.

## ONB-4 30-60-90 Day Milestones

Managers conduct a 30-day check-in focused on onboarding experience and early blockers, a 60-day check-in focused on role expectations and initial project assignments, and a 90-day check-in that includes a formal performance conversation. These milestones are tracked in the performance system separately from this policy corpus. The 90-day mark also aligns with the end of the initial home office stipend window under EXP-2, so managers should confirm with hybrid and remote new hires around this time that their home office setup is complete.

## ONB-5 Manager Responsibilities

Before a new hire's start date, managers must: confirm the new hire's work arrangement classification with People Operations, assign an onboarding buddy from the team, prepare a first-week project or learning plan, and block time on day one for a welcome conversation. Managers are also responsible for confirming the new hire's registered work location matches their actual work location, which affects tax withholding under TAX-2 and TAX-4, and for setting expectations early about the PTO request process (PTO-3) and the manager's own typical approach to approving early PTO requests, so new hires do not need to guess.

## ONB-6 New Hire Paperwork and Compliance

New hires must complete Form I-9 within 3 business days of their start date as required by federal law, complete state and federal tax withholding forms before their first payroll cycle, and, for roles involving client data, complete any client-specific confidentiality agreements identified by the engagement lead. Incomplete I-9 documentation beyond the 3-business-day window may result in suspension of work authorization pending resolution, per federal requirements outside the Company's discretion. People Operations tracks I-9 completion status centrally and follows up directly with any new hire approaching the deadline without complete documentation.

## ONB-7 Onboarding for Remote and Out-of-State New Hires

New hires whose registered work location is a Registered State under TAX-2 but who are not near a Company office follow the same onboarding process described above, with I-9 verification completed through the remote notary service referenced in ONB-2 rather than in person. New hires should not begin working from a location other than the one confirmed during pre-start preparation without following the standard Remote Work Location Change process in RW-3, even during their first weeks of employment; onboarding does not create any special exception to that process, since a brand-new employee's work location carries the same tax and registration implications under TAX-1 through TAX-4 as any other employee's location.

Recruiting and People Operations coordinate before an offer is extended to confirm that a candidate's stated work location is a Registered State, or, if not, to flag the gap to Finance early enough that a registration decision (or a candidate conversation about relocating to a Registered State) can happen before the offer is finalized rather than after a start date has already been set.

## ONB-8 Onboarding Buddy Program

Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy from their team, distinct from their manager, who serves as an informal first point of contact for day-to-day questions during the first 90 days — the kind of practical questions (which internal channel to ask about a specific tool, how the team typically schedules PTO around a project deadline) that do not require an HR ticket but benefit from a peer perspective. The onboarding buddy is not responsible for approvals or policy interpretation; buddy assignments are informal and are tracked by the manager, not by People Operations, since the goal is peer support rather than a formal reporting relationship.

## ONB-9 Common New Hire Questions

The following questions come up frequently enough during onboarding that they are addressed here directly, though the answer in each case is governed by the referenced policy, not by this section:

- **"When can I use PTO?"** PTO begins accruing from the hire date per PTO-2, and can be requested as soon as a positive balance exists, subject to the standard request process in PTO-3. There is no separate onboarding waiting period for PTO beyond the accrual mechanics themselves.
- **"When does my health coverage start?"** Per BEN-3, coverage begins the first of the month following 30 days of continuous full-time employment, which in practice usually means the first of the second full calendar month after the hire date.
- **"Can I work from a different state than the one in my offer letter?"** Only if that state is a Registered State under TAX-2 and the change is reported per TAX-4, or if a longer-term move to a non-Registered State goes through the Remote Work Location Change process in RW-3; onboarding does not change this requirement.
- **"What laptop will I get, and when?"** Standard equipment is described in EQ-1 and ships to the confirmed work location at least 3 business days before the start date per ONB-1; questions about a specific configuration should go through an IT ticket per EQ-2.
