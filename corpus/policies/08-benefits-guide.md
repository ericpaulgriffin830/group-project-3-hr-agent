# Benefits Guide

## BEN-1 Eligibility Overview

Full-time employees (scheduled for 30 or more hours per week) are eligible for the full Northbridge benefits package described in this guide. Part-time employees and contractors have more limited eligibility, described in BEN-7. Benefits eligibility is recorded in each employee's benefits record (see mock data schema `benefits_elections`) and reflects employment type as of the record's effective date. An employee's benefits eligibility can change if their employment type changes (for example, a part-time employee moving to full-time), in which case the waiting period rules in BEN-3 apply from the effective date of the classification change, not from the employee's original hire date.

## BEN-2 Medical, Dental, and Vision Plans

Northbridge offers a choice of two medical plans (a high-deductible health plan with a Company-funded Health Savings Account contribution, and a PPO plan), one dental plan, and one vision plan, all administered through a national insurance carrier. The Company pays 80% of employee-only premiums and 60% of dependent premiums across all plans. Plan documents with detailed coverage terms are available in the benefits portal and are outside the scope of this policy corpus; this guide covers eligibility and process only, not specific coverage details such as deductible amounts, copays, or covered services, which can change independently of this policy on the insurance carrier's renewal schedule.

## BEN-3 Waiting Period and Effective Dates

Medical, dental, and vision coverage becomes effective on the first day of the month following 30 days of continuous full-time employment. An employee hired on any day of a month will generally have coverage begin on the first of the second full month after hire (for example, an employee hired on March 10 has coverage effective May 1). There is no waiting period for retirement plan eligibility (see BEN-4). During the waiting period, an employee who needs medical care can generally elect COBRA-style continuation from a prior employer's plan if recently separated from that employer, though this is a matter between the employee and their prior employer's plan administrator rather than something Northbridge manages.

## BEN-4 Retirement Plan and Employer Match

All full-time employees are eligible to enroll in the Company's 401(k) plan starting on their first day of employment. Northbridge matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of eligible compensation, with immediate vesting, meaning an employee owns 100% of both their own contributions and the Company match from day one, with no vesting schedule to wait out. Enrollment and contribution changes can be made at any time through the retirement plan provider's portal, not only during open enrollment, since retirement contribution elections are not subject to the same qualifying-event restrictions described in BEN-8 for medical, dental, and vision elections.

## BEN-5 Flexible Spending and Health Savings Accounts

Employees enrolled in the high-deductible health plan are eligible for a Health Savings Account (HSA) and receive an annual Company contribution of $600 (individual) or $1,200 (family coverage), deposited in two installments (typically at the start of the plan year and mid-year). Employees on the PPO plan may instead enroll in a Healthcare Flexible Spending Account (FSA), since IRS rules do not permit both an HSA and a general-purpose Healthcare FSA to be held simultaneously. A Dependent Care FSA is available regardless of medical plan choice, since it is a separate account governed by different IRS rules than the healthcare accounts. FSA and HSA elections are made during open enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event (see BEN-8); unlike the 401(k) in BEN-4, FSA elections generally cannot be changed outside of these windows because FSAs are subject to IRS "use it or lose it" and mid-year election change restrictions that do not apply to retirement contributions.

## BEN-6 Life and Disability Insurance

Northbridge provides Basic Life and AD&D insurance equal to one times annual base salary at no cost to full-time employees, with the option to purchase additional voluntary life insurance for the employee and dependents at group rates. Short-term and long-term disability coverage is provided at no cost to full-time employees after the same 30-day waiting period described in BEN-3. Short-term disability benefits may be used in combination with the medical leave provisions in LEAVE-2 and LEAVE-4 to provide partial income replacement during an approved medical leave, though the specific benefit calculation and duration are governed by the disability plan documents rather than by this guide or by the Leave of Absence Policy.

## BEN-7 Part-Time and Contractor Benefits

Part-time employees scheduled for 20 or more hours per week are eligible for the retirement plan described in BEN-4 and for voluntary (employee-paid) life insurance, but are not eligible for Company-subsidized medical, dental, or vision coverage, or for Company HSA/FSA contributions. Part-time employees scheduled for fewer than 20 hours per week, and all contractors, are not eligible for any benefits under this guide. A part-time employee whose scheduled hours change should confirm with People Operations whether the change affects their benefits eligibility tier, since the 20-hour threshold in this section is evaluated based on currently scheduled hours, not an annual average.

## BEN-8 Qualifying Life Events and Open Enrollment

Outside of the annual open enrollment period each November, benefits elections can only be changed within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage, divorce, birth or adoption of a child, or loss of other coverage. Employees should submit a benefits change request through the HR portal within this 30-day window, along with supporting documentation of the qualifying event (such as a marriage certificate or birth certificate); changes requested after 30 days must wait until the next open enrollment period, consistent with standard IRS rules governing employer-sponsored benefit plans, which Northbridge does not have discretion to waive on a case-by-case basis.

## BEN-9 Frequently Encountered Situations

- A new hire starting on March 10 asks when their medical coverage begins. Per BEN-3, coverage begins the first of the second full month after hire, which is May 1.
- An employee on the PPO plan asks whether they can also open an HSA. Per BEN-5, HSAs are only available to employees enrolled in the high-deductible health plan; a PPO-enrolled employee should instead consider the Healthcare FSA.
- A part-time employee scheduled for 24 hours per week asks whether they can enroll in the medical plan. Per BEN-7, they are eligible for the retirement plan and voluntary life insurance, but not for Company-subsidized medical, dental, or vision coverage, since 24 hours per week is below the 30-hour full-time threshold in BEN-1.
- An employee wants to add a newborn to their medical plan in June, outside open enrollment. Per BEN-8, the birth of a child is a qualifying life event, so the employee has 30 days from the birth to submit the change with supporting documentation.
