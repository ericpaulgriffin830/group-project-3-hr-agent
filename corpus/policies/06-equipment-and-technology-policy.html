Equipment and Technology Policy

Document ID: EQUIPMENT · Version 2.5 · Effective 2026-01-05 · Owner: IT · Applies to: All employees and contractors

EQ-1 Standard Issue Equipment

All new employees receive a Company-issued laptop (macOS or Windows, by role and team standard), a laptop stand, a keyboard and mouse, and one external monitor for hybrid and remote employees. Onsite employees may request a monitor for their desk through IT. Devices ship with full-disk encryption, endpoint protection, and mobile device management enrollment pre-configured, satisfying the encryption requirement in SEC-4. Contractors engaged for an extended engagement may also be issued standard equipment at the discretion of the engaging manager and IT, coordinated through the same request process described in EQ-2; contractor equipment issuance does not, however, extend PTO, benefits, or other employee-only entitlements described elsewhere in this corpus.

Equipment specifications are reviewed annually and may vary by role; for example, employees in data engineering roles working with large local datasets may receive a higher-memory configuration than the standard consulting laptop build. Role-specific specifications are maintained by IT separately from this policy and do not require a policy update to change.

EQ-2 Requesting Additional or Replacement Equipment

Additional monitors, docking stations, headsets, or accessories can be requested through an IT ticket with manager approval. Replacement equipment due to normal wear or failure is provided at no cost; replacement due to loss, theft, or damage is handled under EQ-6. Requests for non-standard equipment (for example, a specialized graphics workstation) require a documented business justification and Department Head approval in addition to manager approval, since non-standard equipment falls outside the equipment budget assumptions used in standard planning.

Standard turnaround for an approved accessory request is 3 to 5 business days for domestic shipping; requests involving an approved international remote work arrangement under RW-4 should be submitted with additional lead time given potential customs delays.

EQ-3 Ownership and Return of Equipment

All equipment issued under this policy remains Company property. Employees must return all Company-issued equipment within 5 business days of their last day of employment, using a prepaid shipping label provided by IT for remote employees. Equipment not returned within 30 days may be treated as unreturned Company property and its replacement cost may be deducted from final pay to the extent permitted by applicable state law. IT sends a return reminder at day 10 and day 20 after separation if equipment has not yet been returned or its return has not been scheduled.

EQ-4 Personal Device Use (BYOD)

Personal devices may be used to access email and calendar through the Company's mobile device management application, which enforces a passcode and remote-wipe capability for Company data. Personal devices must not be used to access or store Confidential data as defined in SEC-3, and must not be used as the primary work device in place of Company-issued equipment. An employee who prefers to use a personal device for general productivity work alongside their Company-issued laptop may do so for Public or Internal data, consistent with SEC-2, but Confidential data access remains restricted to Company-managed devices regardless of personal preference.

EQ-5 Software Installation and Licensing

Employees may install standard productivity and development tools from the IT-approved software catalog without additional approval. Software not on the catalog, including free or trial software, requires IT review before installation on a Company device to confirm licensing compliance and security posture. This review is also required before expensing any software subscription over $50/month under EXP-3. IT's review considers both the software vendor's security practices and, per SEC-7, whether the tool involves any AI-assisted processing of Company or client data that would require the additional AI tool review described there.

EQ-6 Loss, Theft, and Damage

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours per SEC-6 so the device can be remotely locked and wiped. A police report is required for stolen equipment before a replacement is issued. The first instance of accidental damage or loss in a rolling 24-month period is replaced at no cost to the employee; a second instance in the same period may require the employee to cover a portion of the replacement cost, at manager and IT discretion, considering the circumstances of both incidents.

EQ-7 Equipment for Approved Temporary and International Remote Work

Standard equipment issuance and return rules under EQ-1 and EQ-3 are not affected by a temporary Remote Work Location Change or International Remote Work Exception approved under RW-3 or RW-4; the employee continues using their standard Company-issued equipment. The only additional consideration is the optional loaner device referenced in RW-5 and RW-7 for higher-risk travel, which is a separate, temporary issuance on top of (not a replacement for) the employee's standard equipment, and is returned upon the employee's return per the same timeline logic as EQ-3.

EQ-8 Equipment Refresh Cycle

Laptops are refreshed on a standard 3-year cycle, tracked by IT based on issuance date rather than requiring an employee request. Monitors and accessories are refreshed on an as-needed basis when they fail or no longer meet role requirements, rather than on a fixed cycle, since these items generally have a longer useful life than a primary laptop and their failure modes are more variable. An employee approaching their 3-year laptop refresh date is contacted proactively by IT with configuration options for the replacement device; no action is required from the employee beyond confirming preferences and a convenient time for the equipment swap.

EQ-9 Shared and Loaner Equipment Pool

Each office maintains a small pool of loaner laptops and conference-room equipment for short-term needs, such as a visiting employee from another office, a new hire whose standard equipment has been delayed in shipping, or the international-travel loaner device referenced in EQ-7. Loaner equipment from this pool is checked out through an IT ticket and is expected to be returned within 10 business days unless a longer period is explicitly agreed with IT; loaner equipment is configured to the same security standard as standard-issue equipment under EQ-1, including full-disk encryption and mobile device management enrollment.
